# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is competent and acting in good faith. Critique the code, not the
person.

## Expected behaviour

- Give feedback on the work, with a reason and ideally a suggestion.
- Accept feedback on your own work the same way.
- Respect that people contribute across time zones, languages, and levels of experience. Someone
  writing in their second language is not being terse at you.
- Assume a question is a real question, not a challenge.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, harassment, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption, bad-faith argument, or dismissing someone for not already knowing something.

## Reporting

Email **ehtisham@eprecisio.com**. Reports stay confidential. I will read it, respond, and act
proportionately, which may mean editing or removing content, or blocking an account.

I am one person maintaining these repositories, not a foundation with a committee. I will handle
reports honestly and promptly, but there is no formal appeals process.

## Scope

Applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and anywhere else these projects are
represented.
